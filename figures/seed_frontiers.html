on the frontier dominated UoT, held out of the seeds

UoT is deliberately absent from every seed set. On Guess Number it reaches 100% — the known best — by keeping the candidate set in Python and playing the last survivor. Seeded, a proposer could read it and copy it, and the search would prove nothing. Held out, it becomes the target: the question is whether a searched harness finds that mechanism on its own, and at what cost.

Its position is drawn from the test split, since it was not re-run here. On Guess Number that is 801,262 tokens an episode, of which 619,971 are generated — the model writes the surviving candidates out turn after turn, which is why one run over 100 episodes takes about nine hours. Cost is counted as input plus output for exactly this reason: by input alone UoT looks 8.6× the cheapest seed when it is really 38×.

Accuracy means a different thing per task: exact match on Guess Number, naming the right suspect out of five on Detective Cases (chance is 20%), and the share of explanations an independent judge calls partially correct on Situation Puzzles. Full equivalence on SP is 0/100 for every method, so the usable signal is the partial tier.
